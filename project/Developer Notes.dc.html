Formatting

  • Money: $ + en-US grouping, always 2 decimals: $38,127.43
  • Dates: dd/mm/yyyy everywhere, including inputs (text inputs, not native date pickers)
  • Rates: 2 decimals + %: 8.99%
  • Loan #: auto-assigned, DC-1000 upwards, never reused

Calculations

  • Deposit $ = cost × deposit %; Financed = cost − deposit
  • Monthly payment = standard amortisation, rate/12, term months; round to cents, last payment clears balance
  • Origination and Legal/closing = 1% of equipment cost, billed in the closing month
  • Referral fee: billed monthly per payment for Closed loans. The prototype uses a placeholder curve matched to Client A; swap in the bank's real schedule
  • "So far" totals = Closed loans with closing date on or before today. Dead loans are excluded everywhere

Phone layout

Below 760px the sidebar becomes a bottom tab bar, tables become cards (Loans, Overview, Billing), and the projection table scrolls sideways. The add-loan form stays a centred sheet with stacked fields.
